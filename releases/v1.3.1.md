**Sowing Season 1.3.1**

A small fix-up release after 1.3.0.

If your bank's export lists every purchase as "DEBIT CARD PURCHASE" and puts the store name in a separate memo column, the importer now learns the store name — so it stops guessing one fund for every row of that bank. Nothing changes for banks that put the store name in the description. Reports' top vendors follow the same rule.

Maximizing the window no longer pops a harmless "something went wrong" message. The one-time carry-over check at first launch now shows expense corrections and income resets as two lists when both apply. Under Reports' fund table, when transfers between funds are excluded from the totals, a line says so.

No changes to your data or your numbers.
