## What's new

**Your paychecks all make it in.** If you're paid weekly, the import used to mark the next week's identical deposit as a "duplicate" and leave it unticked — so some paychecks never counted. Now a deposit is only treated as a duplicate when it's the same amount within three days of one you already have. Paying your credit card from checking is recognized as a card payment (not spending) for more banks, and a payment you typed by hand is matched to its bank line instead of counting twice.

**Better paycheck numbers.** The setup wizard tells weekly pay from every-two-weeks pay and pre-fills the right number of checks. "Use actual" is only offered once the month is over *and* something actually came in — it can't set your estimate to $0 any more — and it divides by the checks that really arrived. The "recent average" suggestion is averaged over your real deposits. Once a month is over, an income shortfall reads "came in under plan" instead of "still expected".

**Money lands in the right month.** A transfer dated in another month asks before posting there (and is refused if that month doesn't exist yet). After importing a bank file that spans months, you stay on the newest month instead of being dropped into the oldest. And when a late import changes an earlier month's carry-over, a savings goal's suggested amount follows it instead of showing an "overridden" flag you never set.

No changes to how your data is stored. Mac: Settings → Check for updates still opens the downloads page; in-place updating on Mac is waiting on a Mac tester.
