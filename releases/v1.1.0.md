## What's new

**Sowing Season is now available for Mac** (macOS 13 Ventura or newer; Apple Silicon and Intel downloads). Everything works the same as on Windows, and your data stays on your computer. One difference for now: on a Mac, **Settings → Check for updates** opens the downloads page instead of updating in place — that's coming.

On Windows, nothing you'll notice changed. Under the hood, the app moved to a newer version of the framework it's built on.
