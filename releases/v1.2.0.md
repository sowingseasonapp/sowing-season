## What's new

**Import lives where your transactions are.** The sidebar's Import page is gone; there's one "Import CSV…" button on the Transactions page that opens your bank file straight away. The preview now leads with what you're actually about to import — "Importing 35 of 60 rows" and the money in and out for just those rows — with the whole file's totals on a second line as a sanity check. And if a file reaches into a month you haven't started yet, the app says so after the import and offers to start that month and bring the leftover rows in, instead of making you go around again.

**Name your accounts.** Open the account filter on Transactions and pick "Name these accounts…" to call 7741 "Chase checking". The name shows everywhere the number did; the number stays on your rows.

**Categories get a ⋯ menu.** Every category header, open or collapsed, has a ⋯ that renames the category (in every month, so your history stays in one row), reorders it, adds a fund, or removes it when it's empty. Settings lost its "Rename a fund" and "Categories" sections — the fund panel and the Budget page own those now.

**Remove a fund in one step.** Remove now moves whatever's left to a fund you pick and takes the fund off your plan. If it already has transactions this month, it stays visible with a quiet "removing at {next month}" chip and leaves when that month starts — nothing is lost and nothing double-counts.

**Two small safety nets.** A fresh install's first screen has a "Bring it in" link for a budget file from another computer, so you don't have to set up a throwaway budget to reach Restore. And the month-end recap gently reminds you to export a copy when it's been three months or more since you did.

**Smaller fixes.** Confirming an unchanged asset value in AUM now counts as checking it (there's a ✓ per row and "Mark all checked"). The importer's auto-match no longer guesses a fund from two or three letters, and tells one card's annual fee from another's. The Garden status line drops its "0 need attention" segments, Transactions has a plain title, and the delete button is a proper icon.

No changes to how your data is stored. Mac: Settings → Check for updates still opens the downloads page; in-place updating on Mac is waiting on a Mac tester.
