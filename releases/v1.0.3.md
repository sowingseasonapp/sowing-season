## What's new

- **Undo and redo.** Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo your last change and **Ctrl+Y** to redo it (Ctrl+Shift+Z works too), or use the new **Undo / Redo** buttons at the bottom of the sidebar. A message tells you exactly what was undone — "Undid the planned change on 'Groceries'." Works for everything you can change: amounts, transactions, transfers, funds, imports, even starting a new month. History covers your last 50 changes and clears when the app closes.
- **The "Left to allocate" card stays with you.** On the Budget page, the big card now stays pinned at the top while you scroll, so you can watch it move toward $0 as you plan. The little pushpin on the card turns this off or back on, and the app remembers your choice.
