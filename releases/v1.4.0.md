**Sowing Season 1.4.0**

The garden grows up.

A fund that finishes on plan month after month now stands on a little base that gets sturdier at three, nine and eighteen months — perennials, earned by staying on plan, and never lost to a bad month that was only carried in from before. Three small fixtures mark firsts: the first month every dollar had a job, the first month you closed, the first close with every fund on plan. Longer histories earn a greenhouse, a bench and lantern, a brook, a willow and an orchard row, and wildlife comes with them.

Beyond the fence there is now a meadow. Every month you gave adds a wildflower, and it stays. The Tithe fund from setup counts already; any other fund can be marked "This is a giving fund" from its panel. Reports has a new Giving tab — what you planned and what you gave, month by month and over the life of the file — and the month recap says what you gave.

If your budget has no tithe fund, the tithe settings and labels stay out of the way until it does.

No changes to your numbers. Nothing in a month's math moved.
