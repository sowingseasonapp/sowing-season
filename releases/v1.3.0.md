## What's new

**Income gets a real month-end check.** When you start the next month, the app now asks about any paycheck or income line that didn't match its plan — and gives you three honest answers: fix the plan (what came in is what you planned), it's late (carry it into next month and wait one more month for it), or use the actual amount. Money that came in but was never assigned carries into next month and counts toward what you have to allocate. Nothing is guessed after the fact any more, and the old "carry forward" checkbox on income lines is gone — the month-end check replaces it. If an older version had carried something on an income line, the first launch resets it and shows you the list.

**You can't start a month on top of a hole.** "Start next month" is disabled while the month you're closing is over-allocated or an income line is unresolved. It tells you how much to fix and opens the Budget page. There's no "start anyway" for that one.

**Make it back over time.** A fund that's carrying red from earlier months can get a plan: make it back over 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months. The amount is added to the fund's monthly plan, counts against what you have to allocate (so you see the trade-off before you commit), and the plan ends itself when the fund is back at zero. Not offered on the tithe — it follows your income instead.

**Reports replaces Year Overview.** Two pages: This Month (the month's headline, plan versus reality, every fund against last month and your average, what changed, top vendors, what rolled forward) and This Year (income and spending by month, where the money went, the category grid at a size that fits the window, planned versus spent per fund with "over in n of m months", biggest purchases, your highest and lowest income months, and any fund's history). The month recap you see when closing a month now reads from the same place, so the numbers always agree.

**Goals that are done can say so.** A one-time savings goal you've reached can be marked done instead of quietly rolling its target a year forward. A fund with one scheduled bill can be told when the next charge is due and how much is already set aside, so the plan asks for the right share from the start.

**The importer makes a better first guess.** When you switch banks and the new one writes a vendor differently, the importer now suggests the fund that vendor's first word has always gone to — marked "check it" in amber, never green, and never across the income/expense line. Picking a fund on one row applies it to the file's other blank rows from the same vendor. Walmart.com and Wal-mart count as one vendor in Reports.

No changes to how your data is stored. Mac: Settings → Check for updates still opens the downloads page; in-place updating on Mac is waiting on a Mac tester.
